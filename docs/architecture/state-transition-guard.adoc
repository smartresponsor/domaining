= Domaining State Transition Guard
:toc:

Domaining W5 introduces a dedicated state-transition guard for binding lifecycle actions.
The goal is to keep transition policy explicit and reusable instead of duplicating status checks across controllers or services.

== Guarded transitions

[source,text]
----
activate_binding
suspend_binding
remove_binding
prepare_publication
mark_published
withdraw_publication
----

== Current policy

* verified or suspended bindings may be activated;
* verified or active bindings may be suspended;
* verified or suspended bindings may be removed;
* verified or active bindings may prepare publication intent;
* active bindings may be marked as published after publication readiness exists;
* suspended or removed bindings may withdraw publication.

== Boundary

The guard validates business state only. Authorization remains in Accessing/Rolling and runtime publication remains outside Domaining.
