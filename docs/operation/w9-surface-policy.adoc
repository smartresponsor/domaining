= Domaining W9 Surface Policy Hardening
:toc:

Wave 9 adds a policy-report layer above the lifecycle diagnostics.

The diagnostic report answers whether the current domain lifecycle state is internally consistent. The surface policy report answers whether a tenant or surface has a safe and reviewable domain posture.

== Added surfaces

* `GET /domain/policy/surface/{ownerId}`
* `php bin/console domaining:policy:surface-report <ownerId>`

== Canonical constraints

* Domaining remains the owner of custom-domain lifecycle and policy.
* Administering may display the policy report but does not own the policy.
* Runtime providers may consume the report but do not decide tenant ownership.
* Cloudflare remains a recommendation/provider hint only, not a required dependency.
